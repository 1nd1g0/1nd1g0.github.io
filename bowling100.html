HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS — TOUCH / MOUSE

1. PLACE: drag left / right to set where the ball starts, then tap to lock. 2. AIM: drag to set the shot angle, tap to lock. 3. THROW: flick UP toward the pins — the flick's travel and speed set the power, and leaning left / right as you release puts hook on the ball.

CONTROLS — KEYBOARD

←→ (or A / D) steps the ball through PLACE → AIM → THROW, ENTER or SPACE locks a step. On the throw step, hold SPACE to charge power (a longer hold is a harder hit) and release to throw; ←→ while holding adds spin. Tab reaches the on-screen buttons, Space or Enter presses them.

THE RULES — CLASSIC 10-PIN

Ten frames; each frame gets up to two balls, three in the 10th if your first two go well. Clear all ten with the first ball = STRIKE: it scores 10 plus a bonus of whatever your next two balls knock down. Clear the rest with the second ball = SPARE: 10 plus the next ball as bonus. Pins left standing after two balls count as themselves. A gutter ball scores nothing and the pins are swept clear between balls. Highest total after ten frames wins; 300 is the perfect game.

100-pin mode spreads a full ten-row rack across a wide lane for chaos; the score sheet stays on the classic 10-pin scale.
