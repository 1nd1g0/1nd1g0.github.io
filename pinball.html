Table features

Every seed builds a unique machine: pop bumpers, slingshots, a tinted-glass ramp, a spinner lane, a drop-target bank, top rollover lanes and a saucer. Flipper buttons change the blinking skill-shot lane while the ball waits on the plunger. Both orbit returns are welded to the outer wall and feed an inlane, and every generated feature is clearance-solved so no wedge can trap the ball.

Rules

Light all top lanes to raise the multiplier. Clear the drop bank to light LOCK at the saucer — lock two balls for 3-ball multiball, where every ramp shot pays the jackpot. End-of-ball bonus × multiplier. An extra ball is awarded at the seed's secret score. Three quick nudges = TILT.
