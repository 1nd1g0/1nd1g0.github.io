❓ Features & Modes

🎮 Non-Classic Features

GUIDELINEGhost Piece — translucent preview showing where the piece will land.

GUIDELINEHold Piece — press C to reserve a piece for later (once per drop).

GUIDELINE5-Next Queue — see the next 5 pieces to plan ahead.

GUIDELINE7-Bag Randomizer — every 7 pieces contain each shape exactly once (fair distribution).

GUIDELINESRS Wall Kicks — Super Rotation System with 5-test kick tables for I and JLSTZ pieces.

GUIDELINET-Spin Detection — rotating a T-piece into a tight corner counts as a T-Spin (bonus points).

GUIDELINEBack-to-Back (B2B) — consecutive Tetrises or T-Spins give 1.5× score.

GUIDELINECombo System — consecutive line clears build a combo multiplier.

EFFECTZone — press V when meter is full: time slows to 20% speed for 5 seconds, board glows blue.

TGMGrand Master Grade — ranking from 9 up to GM, based on score + style bonuses.

TGM20G Gravity — pieces fall instantly at high levels (TGM-style acceleration).

TGMLock Delay + Move Reset — piece locks after 500ms on ground OR after 15 move/rotate inputs (whichever first).

99/PuyoGarbage Lines — in Garbage mode, incomplete rows with a gap are injected from below periodically.

CLASSICDAS/ARR — Delayed Auto Shift (133ms) + Auto Repeat Rate (50ms) for smooth horizontal movement.

🎲 Game Modes

🎮 Marathon — classic endless play. Level up every 10 lines. Gravity increases gradually.

⚡ Sprint — clear 40 lines as fast as possible. Timer stops at completion.

🏆 Grand Master — TGM-style. 20G gravity kicks in early. Earn the GM grade through skillful play.

🌀 Zone — charge your Zone meter by clearing lines. Activate for slow-motion power.

⏱ Ultra — score as many points as possible in 2 minutes.

💀 Garbage Battle — survive against periodic garbage lines. Clear lines to delay the next wave.
